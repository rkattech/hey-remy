Questions, answered.

What if Remy gets something wrong?
Nothing is sent, booked, or paid without your approval. You see the exact email or booking and press-and-hold to OK it. If a draft isn't right, edit it or just tell Remy in the chat.
What's the "beta" part?
Remy runs through TestFlight, Apple's app for trying new apps early. Getting in takes two taps: install TestFlight, then Remy. Sign in with Google and you're talking - nothing to configure.
What does it cost?
Free during the beta. We'll share pricing well before you'd ever be asked to pay.
Is my data private?
Remy reads your email and calendar to help you, and for nothing else. Never sold, never used for ads, never used to train AI models - and you can see and delete everything it knows, any time.
How is this different from ChatGPT or Siri?
ChatGPT can tell you about your dentist. Remy books the appointment. Siri reads one email. Remy triages your whole inbox. An assistant that does real work, not a chatbot that talks about it.
Do I need to install anything weird?
Just the iPhone app. No CLI, no browser extension, no daemon running on your laptop.
When can I try it?
We're onboarding in small batches to keep the experience tight. Join the waitlist and we'll reach out as we open the next cohort.
