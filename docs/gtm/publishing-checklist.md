## De-AI pass v2 (added 2026-08-20 after Pangram flagged the incidents post 4/7 AI)

The word-list grep is the shallow half. The structural tells are what detectors and
readers actually catch. Before founder read, verify the draft against these:

- No parallel-formula answer boxes ("X: ... / Y: ... / The one question:"). If the
  answer box stays, it's plain prose.
- No setup-payoff fragments as openers ("Not a scare piece. A dated, sourced record.")
- No aphorism one-liners manufactured for quotability ("deleted can't leak").
  One is fine if it's doing work; three is a template.
- No numbered sections that all open with the same sentence shape.
- Sentence lengths must be uneven; read a paragraph aloud - if it has a drumbeat, break it.
- Connective tissue in first person, in the /why register (short declaratives,
  concrete referents from the founder's PRINTED facts only - never invent anecdotes).
- Quote-dense sections read human; keep quotes long and let them carry sections.
- Founder may spot-check in Pangram (limited credits): target = no High-confidence
  AI segments on the connective tissue.
